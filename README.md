# optionstrat

A comprehensive options trading dashboard designed for investors and traders to explore, learn, and execute sophisticated options strategies.

## Overview

optionstrat is an interactive platform that empowers users to understand and leverage mathematically complex option strategies across various risk profiles. Whether you're a beginner exploring foundational concepts or an advanced trader implementing multi-leg strategies, optionstrat provides the tools and insights needed to make informed trading decisions.

## Key Features

### 📚 Options Education
- Learn about options fundamentals and advanced concepts
- Explore various option strategies with visual explanations
- Understand Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta, Rho) and their impact on pricing
- Interactive tutorials on risk management and strategy selection

### 📊 Strategy Explorer
- Browse multiple pre-configured option strategies (spreads, straddles, strangles, etc.)
- Analyze payoff diagrams and profit/loss scenarios
- Calculate strategy performance across different market conditions
- Filter strategies by risk level, complexity, and market outlook

### 🔌 Brokerage Integration
- Connect your brokerage account to execute trades directly
- Real-time pricing and market data integration
- One-click trade execution on analyzed strategies
- Portfolio tracking and performance monitoring

### 👥 Social Features
- Share strategies with the trading community
- Collaborate and discuss options trading ideas
- View others' strategy analysis and results
- Build your trading network and learn from experienced traders
